## Motivation

Often people struggle with learning foreign languages, especially at the point after they learn the basics. Most times they can not advance pass that plateu of progress not because they lack the ability to do so but because they simply do not know how to, e.g. what method to use.

Generally the best method of learning a foreign language after mastering the basics is to gain experience first hand, whether through talking with others or listening to a native speaking it.

ChiLearn offers that method through a trained ChatBot that would feel like chatting to a real native person.

## Get started

To get started just press the start button on the homepage.
